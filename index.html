⚙️ PID Tuning Quick Guide

Defaults work for most setups! Only change if:

  • Speed oscillates → Decrease Kp (try 0.30)
  • Won't reach target → Increase Ki (try 0.15)
  • Overshoots target → Increase Kd (try 0.10)
  • Too slow to respond → Increase Kp (try 0.70)

💡 Tip: Try adjusting Speed Smoothing first - often fixes issues without touching PID!
